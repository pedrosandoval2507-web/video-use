# Instructions for Claude

## Pedro's videos (Reels / vertical talking-head)

Whenever the user asks to edit a video (especially a vertical video of them talking to the camera), **read `reels/EDITING-STYLE.md` before anything else** and follow that format. It is the creator's approved style, built from feedback on real videos:
- text only inside the Reels safe zones; the face never gets covered,
- the person **cut out in front** of images and cards,
- light-blue captions, phrase by phrase, with red emphasis words and words that grow in,
- **mixed fonts for emphasis** (bold sans + italic serif on the key word) and **minimalist icons** for abstract ideas,
- a suspense hook, images with a woosh, real data with a source,
- **upbeat/motivational music** (never melancholic).

The step-by-step workflow is in the `reels-editor` skill (`.claude/skills/reels-editor/SKILL.md`), and the scripts are in `reels/scripts/`.

The `video-use` skill (root `SKILL.md`) still applies to its production-correctness rules; on style, `reels/EDITING-STYLE.md` takes precedence.

Reply to the user in Portuguese (pt-BR).
